
Act as a senior Full Stack Web Developer with 15+ years of experience in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP/Python, and MySQL. Design and develop a modern, fully responsive, professional Fruit & Dry Fruits Shop Management System.

## Project Overview

Develop a complete e-commerce web application for a Fruit & Dry Fruits Shop where:

* Customers can create an account, log in, browse products, add items to their cart, and place orders.
* The Admin can securely log in, manage products, categories, orders, customers, and inventory through a dedicated admin dashboard.

The application should have a premium, modern UI/UX with smooth animations, attractive colors, and mobile responsiveness.
